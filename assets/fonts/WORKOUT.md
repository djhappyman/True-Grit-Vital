# True Grit family fonts

These runtime fonts are unchanged copies of the sibling Workout tracker fonts.
Plus Jakarta Sans supplies the app UI; JetBrains Mono supplies numeric/code roles.
Weights match the sibling app: 400, 500, 600 and 700. All are bundled for offline use.
Noto Sans remains available for existing document/export rendering.

The font copyright and OFL link were checked in every copied file. Full SIL Open
Font License 1.1 texts are bundled beside the fonts and in the app notice collection.
Primary notices: https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/plusjakartasans/OFL.txt
and https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/jetbrainsmono/OFL.txt.
